GhostWord Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-27

App: GhostWord for Android (app ID dev.auxon.ghostword)

This page: https://cyrillical00.github.io/ghostword-site/privacy/

Contact: ghostword@auxon.dev

The short version

GhostWord keeps everything on your phone. It never sends anything to the developer or to a server, and it has no account, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting. Things leave your phone only when you share your picks, or through your phone's own features, like Android backup and the voice that reads words aloud. This page explains each one. You can delete your history whenever you like. GhostWord is for entertainment.

Who we are

GhostWord is made by Auxon Technology LLC, a California limited liability company ("Auxon", "we"). Auxon is the developer shown on GhostWord's Google Play listing. You can reach us at ghostword@auxon.dev with any question about this policy or about your data.

What GhostWord stores on your phone

GhostWord saves a few things in its private storage area on your phone:

  • Your sessions. For each session: the question you typed (if you typed one), when it started and ended, how long it ran, the word list and word speed it used, every word that appeared and when, and how you marked each word.
  • Your settings. The word interval, your default word list, your voice settings, whether "Stir with motion sensors" is on, and whether you've finished the intro.
  • A signing key. The first time it needs one, GhostWord makes a random key and keeps it in Android's secure storage. The app uses it to sign a record of each finished session. The key stays on your phone and isn't backed up.

The question you type stays in your session record on your phone. GhostWord never sends it anywhere. If Android backup is on, it's backed up with the rest of your history (see Backups).

What GhostWord doesn't do

  • It doesn't send data to us, to a server or to any third party on its own. The app's own code makes no network requests. The only thing GhostWord hands to another app is what you choose to share (see Sharing your picks).
  • It has no account or sign-in, no ads, no analytics and no crash-reporting tools.
  • It doesn't use your location, microphone, camera, contacts, photos or files.
  • It doesn't save or send the motion and compass readings it mixes into the word draw (see Motion and compass sensors).
  • It doesn't track you across apps or websites.

Spoken words

GhostWord reads each new word aloud with your phone's built-in text-to-speech engine. It hands the engine only the word, never your question or your ratings.

You choose the voice engine in Android's text-to-speech settings. That engine belongs to your phone's maker or to the engine's developer, not to us. Some engines can use online voices. If yours does, the engine handles the word under its own privacy policy. To keep speech on your phone, pick an offline voice in your text-to-speech settings, or switch off "Speak words aloud" in GhostWord's settings.

Motion and compass sensors

Starting with version 1.1, GhostWord can read your phone's accelerometer (its motion sensor) and magnetometer (its compass sensor) while a session runs on screen. It mixes each reading into the random draw that picks the next word, then drops it. Every word stays equally likely, so the readings never make a word more or less likely, and they never bring a word sooner or later.

GhostWord keeps the mix only in your phone's memory and drops it when the session screen closes. It never saves the readings, never writes them to a log and never sends them anywhere. Reading these sensors needs no Android permission. The sensors stop when you pause, leave the app or end the session. "Stir with motion sensors" is on unless you switch it off in GhostWord's settings. With it off, GhostWord opens no sensor at all.

Backups

If Android backup is on, Android may copy GhostWord's history and settings to your Google account, and restore them when you set up a new phone or move them straight to a new one. Android and Google handle that under Google's own privacy policy. GhostWord never sees it. The signing key isn't included, so a restored phone makes a new one. You can turn backup off or delete backups in your phone's Google and system settings.

The privacy policy link

The Settings screen has a Privacy policy link. It opens this page in your phone's browser. That's an ordinary web visit made by your browser, and the website's host may log it the way any website does. GhostWord sends nothing with it.

Google Play

You get GhostWord from Google Play. Google gives us totals such as installs, crash and not-responding counts (from people who share usage and diagnostics with Google) and any review you choose to post. We never get your questions, words or ratings. Google's own privacy policy covers what Google collects.

Sharing your picks

The report screen has a Share button. It makes a picture, or a short text if a picture can't be made, with the words you marked, the name of the word list, the date and a small "Made with GhostWord" line. It never includes your question. GhostWord saves the picture in its private cache on your phone and opens Android's share sheet. You choose where it goes, and nothing is sent until you do. The app you pick handles it under its own privacy policy, and GhostWord can't see where it went. Android can clear the cache at any time. Anything you screenshot or screen-record yourself goes where you send it, too.

Children

GhostWord isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children. The app doesn't send us data, so there's nothing for us to hold or delete.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your phone until you delete it. We don't keep a copy.

  • One session: open History, swipe the session to the left, and confirm.
  • Everything: open Android Settings, then Apps, then GhostWord, then Storage and cache, and tap Clear storage (some phones say Clear data). Uninstalling GhostWord removes it too.
  • Backups: if Android backup was on, delete the old backup in your Google account's backup settings.

Because we hold none of your data, we can't look it up, export it or delete it for you. You control all of it on your phone.

Security

GhostWord's data lives in the app's private storage, which other apps can't normally read. The signing key sits in Android's secure storage. Keep your phone locked and up to date.

For entertainment

GhostWord is a game of words and atmosphere, made for entertainment. It isn't advice of any kind, including medical, mental health, legal or financial advice, and it isn't a substitute for talking to a real person. If you're having a hard time, please reach out to someone you trust or to a professional.

Your rights

Privacy laws in some places give you rights over data a company holds about you. We hold none, because nothing is sent to us. You're welcome to ask us anything at ghostword@auxon.dev.

If a future version of GhostWord changes how it handles data, for example by connecting to the internet, we'll update this page first and update the Data safety section on Google Play to match. The effective date at the top shows when this page last changed.
