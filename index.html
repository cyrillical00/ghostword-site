GhostWord

A playful word séance for dark rooms and late nights.

Ask a question, then listen as words drift in one at a time. GhostWord draws words at random from its own word lists, reads each one aloud, and lets you tap the ones that land. When you're done, you get a list of your picks.

For entertainment. Words are picked at random from GhostWord's word lists.

Private by default

No account, no ads and no analytics. GhostWord works offline and keeps your questions, picks and history on your phone.
